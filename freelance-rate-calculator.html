Rule of thumb: If you cannot explain the contingency line in one sentence, raise hours instead of hiding risk in a vague discount later.

Simple formula (before tiers)

base = hourly × hours rush = base × (rush% / 100) contingency = base × (contingency% / 100) total = base + rush + contingency + materials

That total is your honest single-option quote. Tiers come next when the client wants choice without a free custom redesign for every budget.

Core / Recommended / Comprehensive

Three packages let buyers self-select. Keep the differences real — scope and outcomes, not just “more hours” with the same deliverable.

  • Core — minimum viable outcome; tight scope; fewer revisions.
  • Recommended — the option you want most clients to pick; best balance.
  • Comprehensive — extras, research, extra rounds, or faster turnaround.

In Quote, multipliers are editable so your Recommended can sit at e.g. 1.0× and Comprehensive at 1.35× of the calculated base — or whatever matches your packaging.

How to use numbers in a proposal

  1. Run the calculator with conservative hours.
  2. Lock a floor you will not go below.
  3. Offer three tiers with clear “included / not included.”
  4. Paste totals into the proposal investment section.
  5. If they ask for cheaper, shrink scope — do not silently cut the floor.
