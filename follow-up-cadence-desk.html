What belongs in a follow-up cadence desk?

Three lists: which leads are still open, which day-index they are on (0/3/7/14), and a short template outline for each step. Every open lead needs a next touch date — otherwise “I’ll nudge later” wins.

Why a fixed cadence beats memory

Bestsellers that promise “follow-up alerts” or “days since last contact” work when the day-index is boring and visible. Memory fails on Friday afternoon. A Day 0 → 3 → 7 → 14 ladder gives you permission to pause or close a lead instead of endless polite ghosts.

Beacon is not a full Lead→Paid CRM. Pair it with Lane when deals need stages and invoices, and with free Roster when you only need a who-to-ping list.

Log every touch. Advance the day-index when a step completes. Without a next_touch date, the board is decoration.

Friday cadence review

  1. Send or reschedule every Due today lead.
  2. Log each touch with template id + outcome.
  3. Advance day_index when a step completes.
  4. Close-loop Day 14 leads.
  5. Park Lost — do not leave Waiting forever.
