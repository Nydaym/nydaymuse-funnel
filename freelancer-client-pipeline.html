What belongs in a freelancer client pipeline?

Four linked lists: who the client is, which deal stage they sit in (Lead → Paid), which project is live, and which invoices are open. You only need one home base — Notion or a spreadsheet — as long as every open deal has a next action and a date.

Stages that match real freelance work

Use short stage names you will actually move:

StageMeaning
LeadFirst contact or inbound interest
QuotePricing shared
ProposalFormal proposal out
NegotiationTerms or price still open
ContractedSigned or deposit agreed
DeliverWork in progress
PaidInvoice collected
LostClosed-lost — park it

Every open deal needs a next action and a due date. Without those two fields, a Kanban board is decoration.

Friday 15-minute review

  1. Move Pipeline cards that changed stage.
  2. Fill next action + due date for every open deal.
  3. Check invoices still marked Sent past their due date.
  4. Update project % complete and hours logged.
  5. Flag Active / Retainer clients with no contact in 14+ days.

Client pipeline questions

No. Pick one home base. Lane includes both formats so you can choose — not so you maintain two sources of truth.
