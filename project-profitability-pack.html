What belongs in a project profitability pack?

Related records for project fees, hours logged and expenses, plus a simple margin / effective hourly summary. Use the workbook or configure the Notion CSVs manually; matching project IDs in a CSV do not create live Notion relations.

LibreOffice input limitation: Use actual numbers in fee, hours, expense and budget fields, and actual dates in date fields. TRUE/FALSE values or logical formula results can import as 1/0 in LibreOffice without an input-check warning. These logical values are unsupported; inspect imported numeric/date columns even when the checks say OK. Real numeric zero and one remain supported where the field permits them.

A project can feel busy and still pay poorly. Freelancers who only track invoices miss the hours and the small expenses that erase the fee. Spreadsheet people and Notion people buy the same idea — fee vs hours vs expenses — then bounce when the tool forces the wrong format.

Margin is Nydaymuse’s $12 dual-format profitability pack: Projects, Time_Log, Expenses, and Rate_Card as Notion CSVs and as an XLSX workbook with a Margin_Rollup. Pick one; the formats do not sync.

Why effective hourly beats vibes

Tracking effective hourly and project margin helps you compare the quoted fee with recorded time and expenses. Margin keeps those records in an XLSX workbook or CSVs for manual Notion setup.

Margin is not a full accounting suite. Use its Time_Log and Expenses sheets to record project hours and spend, and pair it with Ops for invoice aging.

Simple definitions that stick

MetricIdea
Effective hourly(fee − expenses) ÷ hours logged
Simple margin $fee − expenses
Margin %margin $ ÷ fee
At riskmanually assigned status after reviewing hours, budget and margin

Enter the fee basis manually: a fixed fee or an hourly billed total calculated separately. Rate_Card is a reference list; it does not calculate fees or deduct labor cost. All logged hours and expense amounts count, including non-billable time and reimbursable expenses. Any reimbursement revenue in the fee basis must be entered manually and consistently.

Effective hourly is blank at zero total hours; margin percentage is blank at zero fee. Enter a numeric zero when it is intended; a missing fee is an input error. Simple margin excludes your own labor cost, taxes and cash collections. All monetary values are USD, without currency conversion.

Dashboard totals include all statuses, including Pipeline and Lost, and all dates. Dates are recordkeeping fields, not reporting-period filters. Blended effective hourly divides total simple margin by total hours; it does not average project rates.

Friday margin review

  1. Log time for every live project.
  2. Capture expenses the week they hit.
  3. Recalculate, resolve input-check warnings, and review Margin_Rollup. Set At risk status manually where needed.
  4. Update Rate_Card if your floor moved.
  5. Decide: raise fee, cut scope, or decline lookalike work.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use Notion and Excel at the same time?

No. Pick one home base. Margin includes both formats so you can choose.

Is Margin a live Notion duplicate link?

No. Import the four CSVs and set their property types. Keep totals updated manually or configure the documented relations, rollups and formulas, linking each log row to its project. Matching text IDs do not create relations. The page outline does not build a working database or dashboard.

How is Margin different from Ops?

Ops is the deeper money/aging workbook. Margin joins fee + time + expenses into effective hourly and margin, with Time_Log and Expenses sheets included.
