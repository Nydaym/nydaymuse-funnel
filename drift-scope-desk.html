Frequently asked questions

Does Drift send change-order emails for me?

Drift provides email templates to copy and adapt. Review the request, price, and timing, then send the message through your own email account.

How is Drift different from Quote?

Quote helps calculate the initial project estimate. Drift records requests that arrive after the scope is set, so you can decide whether to quote them separately or absorb the work.

Does Drift sync between devices?

Drift stores records in localStorage in the browser you use. It does not provide cloud sync, and clearing browser storage can remove local records.
