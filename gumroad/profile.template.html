GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you begin.

Simple tools should come with simple answers.

Where should I start?

For freelance pricing, try Quote. For client outreach, start with Hook. For late invoices, explore Nudge. The filters above can help you find tools for creative projects and everyday life, too.

What do I get when I choose a tool?

Each product page lists the files, format, and what is included. The collection includes offline HTML tools, PDF resources, spreadsheets, Notion import packs, and AI prompts. Downloads are delivered through Gumroad.

Can I use the tools offline?

The offline HTML tools run locally in your browser once downloaded. PDFs and downloaded workbooks can also be used locally with suitable software. Notion and AI prompt packs use their respective services, so check the product page for requirements.

Do the templates include real client data?

Examples are there to help you get started. Replace sample entries with your own information before using a template for real work.

Where can I find practical guides?

The Nydaymuse guide library covers pricing, proposals, onboarding, invoice follow-ups, and more. You can read the guides without downloading a tool.
