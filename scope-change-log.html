Fee + hours impact

  1. Log every new ask the same day.
  2. Price fee_impact_usd and hours_impact before drawing.
  3. Catch Approvals Pending on Friday review.
  4. Roll Impact by status so pending $ is visible.
  5. Never silently expand the baseline.
