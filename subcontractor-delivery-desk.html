What is inside?

From submitted work to a manual invoice review

  1. Define the fixed-fee package, brief version and exact reference
  2. Record the submitted version and assign concrete checks to its review
  3. Inspect the work and store the reviewed snapshot as values
  4. Reconcile the billed, accepted-work and paid amounts you record
  5. Use the recorded status to decide what needs your attention next

“Ready for manual invoice review” means the recorded checks allow the next human step. It does not establish that a payment is authorized, due, sent or complete.

One project and currency per workbook; one invoice per package; 1–10 requirements per review, including at least one required check. The four tables have 100 prepared rows each, including retained history. Hourly billing, split invoices, tax calculations and retainers are outside this workflow.

Snapshots are editable input records, not signatures, file checksums or tamper-proof audit trails. Reinspect changed files. A change inside an external file with the same recorded version and reference cannot be detected by the workbook.

Before you download

Crew is for the buyer of fixed-fee creative subcontracted work. It is a self-serve, editable recordkeeping download. The files use .xlsx workbooks; no native Microsoft Excel or live Notion import test is claimed. Individual setup, customization and individual usage support are not included. All examples are fictional teaching scenarios.

CSV files require manual import and property setup in Notion. They do not carry Excel formulas, formatting, validation, relations, rollups or automatic status checks. This is not a native one-click Notion template. Excel and Notion do not synchronize.

Crew does not inspect or independently verify submitted files or evidence. It does not establish legal acceptance, approve invoices, determine legal amounts owed, or initiate payments. No legal, contract, tax, worker-classification, accounting or payment advice is provided.
