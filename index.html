Questions before you download

Is this a subscription?

No. Quote, Nudge, and Slip are free. Close, Kickoff, and Ops are $12 each, one-time, with instant download on Gumroad.

What do I actually download?

Quote and Slip are offline HTML files that run in your browser. Nudge is a PDF plus a subject-line swipe file. Close is a pack of 8 prompts for ChatGPT or Claude. Kickoff is a Notion pack with sample Clients / Projects / Intake CSVs. Ops is a ZIP with the workbook and an overdue-lite HTML tool.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse this site or read the guides. Every tool, free or paid, is delivered through Gumroad checkout as an instant download.

Do the free tools send my data anywhere?

Quote runs entirely in your browser and works offline; the numbers you enter stay on your device. Slip is a print-ready HTML layout you fill in and print or save as PDF.

Which one should I start with?

If underpricing is the pain, start with Quote. If chasing invoices is the pain, start with Nudge and grow into Ops. If every new client starts from scratch, Kickoff.
