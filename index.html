GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you start.

Simple tools should come with simple answers.

Is this a subscription?

No. Quote, Nudge, Slip, and Praise are free. Close, Kickoff, and Ops are $12 each, one-time, with instant download on Gumroad.

What do I actually download?

Quote is an offline HTML pack with a rate calculator and printable proposal. Slip is a print-ready HTML receipt. Nudge is a PDF plus subject lines. Praise is an offline testimonial desk. Close includes 8 prompts for ChatGPT or Claude. Kickoff is a Notion pack with sample Clients, Projects, and Intake CSVs. Ops includes a workbook and an overdue HTML tool.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse this site or read the guides. Every tool, free or paid, is delivered through Gumroad checkout as an instant download.

Do the free tools send my data anywhere?

Quote runs entirely in your browser and works offline; the numbers you enter stay on your device. The quick estimate on this page also stays in your browser and is not saved. Slip is a print-ready HTML layout you fill in and print or save as PDF.

Which one should I start with?

If pricing is the hard part, start with Quote. For overdue invoices, try Nudge, then Ops when you want a fuller picture. If each new client means starting from scratch, try Kickoff.
