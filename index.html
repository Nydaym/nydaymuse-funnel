GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you start.

Simple tools should come with simple answers.

Is this a subscription?

No. Quote, Drift, Nudge, Slip, and Praise are free. Close, Scope, Kickoff, and Ops are $12 each; Change is $15; the Client Workflow Bundle is $49 — all one-time downloads on Gumroad.

What do I actually download?

Quote is an offline HTML pack with a rate calculator and printable proposal. Drift is a free scope desk; Change ($15) is the formal Drift upgrade. Slip is a one-page invoice (Excel/Sheets + offline HTML lite for Print→PDF). Nudge is a PDF plus subject lines. Praise is an offline testimonial desk. Close is an expanded AI prompt pack (Cue folded in — Cue, Bound, and Pitch are no longer sold separately). Kickoff is a Notion pack. Ops is a workbook plus overdue tool. Scope includes Bound + Pitch PDFs.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse this site or read the guides. Every tool, free or paid, is delivered through Gumroad checkout as an instant download.

Do the free tools send my data anywhere?

Quote runs entirely in your browser and works offline; the numbers you enter stay on your device. The quick estimate on this page also stays in your browser and is not saved. Slip is a print-ready HTML layout you fill in and print or save as PDF.

Which one should I start with?

If pricing is the hard part, start with Quote. For scope creep, try Drift, then Change when you need a signed order. For overdue invoices, try Nudge, then Ops. If each new client means starting from scratch, try Kickoff. If you want one Lead→Paid board in Notion or Excel, try Lane.
