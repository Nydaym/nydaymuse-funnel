What every freelance proposal template needs

Clients skim. Give them sections they can forward to a boss or finance person without rewriting:

  1. Context — one short paragraph on their situation (their words, not jargon).
  2. Outcomes — what “done” looks like in business terms.
  3. Scope in / out — protect both sides; list what is not included.
  4. Timeline — phases or milestones, not vague “ASAP.”
  5. Investment — one price or three tiers with clear differences.
  6. Assumptions — access, feedback speed, assets, decision owner.
  7. Next step — reply, deposit, or kickoff date.

Pricing tip: Freelancer pricing fails when the proposal only lists hours. Tie the number to outcomes and a floor you will not go below — then offer a smaller scope if budget is real.

Skeleton you can paste today

Subject: Proposal — [Project] for [Client] Hi [Name], Thanks for the call. Here is a concise proposal based on what we discussed. Context [1–2 sentences on their goal and constraint] Outcomes - [Outcome 1] - [Outcome 2] Scope (included) - [Deliverable A] - [Deliverable B] Out of scope - [Item you will not do unless re-scoped] Timeline [Start] → [Milestone] → [Delivery] ([X] weeks, assuming feedback in [N] days) Investment Option A — Core: $[AMOUNT] Option B — Recommended: $[AMOUNT] Option C — Comprehensive: $[AMOUNT] Assumptions [Access, assets, decision owner, revision rounds] Next step If this looks right, reply with your preferred option and I will send the kickoff checklist / invoice for the deposit. Best, [Your name]

Before you send the AI draft

  • Match each deliverable and exclusion to your discovery notes.
  • Recalculate prices and check that package differences match their scope.
  • Confirm dates, revision rounds, and who supplies assets or approves work.
  • Resolve every bracketed unknown and give the client one explicit next step.

Freelancer pricing mistakes proposals make

  • One giant price with no tiers — buyers cannot self-select a smaller yes.
  • Hourly only — invites “how many hours?” debates instead of outcomes.
  • No out-of-scope list — every “quick add” becomes free work.
  • Discount before ask — cutting price in the first draft trains buyers to wait.
  • No next step — a nice PDF that dies in inbox limbo.

Freelance proposal prompt questions

What should I give AI before asking for a proposal?

Provide the client's goal, confirmed deliverables, exclusions, deadline, dependencies, and pricing you have approved. A budget signal is useful context, but it is not a substitute for an agreed scope or your own price calculation.

What if my discovery notes are incomplete?

Ask the model to mark missing details in brackets and produce a short question list. Confirm those details with the client before sending a proposal that commits to them.

Do I need to offer three proposal tiers?

No. One clearly scoped option can be enough. Offer multiple tiers when there are meaningful differences in deliverables, revision rounds, or timing, and check the price of each option yourself.
