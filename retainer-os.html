Rollover rules that stay boring

RuleMeaning
Unused hours roll 1 monthLeftover moves once; then expires
Rollover capMax hours that may enter next month
No rolloverUse-it-or-lose-it within the month

Write the rule once per retainer. Apply it on Month_Close before drafting next invoices.

Month-close checklist

  1. Reconcile hours_used vs deliverable hours.
  2. Apply rollover rule + cap; note expired hours.
  3. Draft next-month invoices on each invoice_day.
  4. Chase anything still Sent past due.
  5. Pause retainers that should not silently continue.
