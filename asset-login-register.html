Why freelancers need a vault map

Kickoff collects assets at the start. Relay revokes at the end. Vault is the ongoing map of where files and access live while work is active.

Pair with Kickoff, Relay, Client CRM, and Harbor for retainer rhythm.

No clear passwords — ever

SAMPLE secret_hint values are literal placeholders like [PLACEHOLDER — not a real password]. Store real secrets in 1Password / Bitwarden / similar. This pack stores pointers (secret_storage) and hints only.

Monthly audit habit

The Audit sheet asks: no clear-text passwords, every Active access has a pointer, closed projects are Revoked or Transferred, and last_audit dates stay fresh.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use Notion and Excel at the same time?

No. Pick one home base. Vault includes both formats so you can choose.

Are there real passwords in the SAMPLE?

No. Every secret field is a placeholder like [PLACEHOLDER — not a real password].

Is Vault a password manager?

No. It is a register of where files and access live, with pointers to your real vault.
