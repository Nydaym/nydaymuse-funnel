Frequently asked questions

Does Praise send testimonial requests automatically?

Praise helps you prepare requests with email templates. You review the text and send it yourself.

Can I publish a generated testimonial draft as a client quote?

Ask the client to review and approve the wording first. A draft is a starting point, not evidence of what a client said. Record their permission for the places where you plan to publish it.

Can I back up my Praise records?

Praise stores records locally and includes JSON export. Keep an exported backup before clearing browser storage or changing devices.
