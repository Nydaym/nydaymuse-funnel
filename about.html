About Nydaymuse

Site information updated September 20, 2026

Nydaymuse makes downloadable productivity tools for freelancers, creators, and everyday organization. The range includes offline HTML desks, spreadsheets, Notion packs, and AI prompt packs. This website explains the tools and their workflows; the Nydaymuse Gumroad shop handles downloads and purchases.

What the tools help with

  • Quote calculates project estimates and includes a printable proposal. Close supplies proposal and pricing prompts.
  • Kickoff organizes client onboarding. Drift tracks scope changes, and Praise helps prepare testimonial requests.
  • Nudge provides payment reminder templates. Ops organizes invoice and client records.
  • The guide directory also covers job searches, photography, podcasts, video planning, and family logs.

About these guides

The guides are published by Nydaymuse and describe Nydaymuse products. Product links lead to our own shop. Examples, sample rows, and mock previews illustrate a workflow; they are not customer results or testimonials. Replace sample data and check every draft before using it.

Check each product's Gumroad listing for its current price, included files, and purchase terms. A guide's update date refers to the page, not a promise that the downloadable product changed on that date.

Downloads and data

Offline HTML tools open in your browser after download. Where a tool uses browser storage, records are tied to that browser and device; clearing storage can remove them. Keep a separate backup using the export feature described for that tool. Download and purchase links require internet access.

Files you import into Notion or paste into an AI service are then handled by that service. An offline companion does not make the separate Notion or AI workflow local. Prompt packs provide text to use with your own AI account.

Choosing a download

Start with the guide for the task you need to complete and check the listed format. These are self-service downloads; check the product listing before purchasing if you need setup help, integrations, or a particular workflow.
