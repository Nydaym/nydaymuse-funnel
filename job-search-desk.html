How to run a weekly job hunt

  1. Research — shortlist roles that match your floor title and location constraints.
  2. Apply — log Company, Role, Channel, Status, Salary signal, and the next action date.
  3. Prep — rehearse six STAR stories before any live screen.
  4. Follow through — send thank-you or nudge emails within 24–48 hours, not “someday.”

Hunt’s weekly checklist is printable so the rhythm stays visible on your desk — not buried in a task manager with fifty other projects.

STAR stories without blank-page panic

Interviewers ask for conflict, ambiguity, and influence. Writing stories the night before a loop usually produces vague answers. Hunt keeps a small bank of templates with bracket placeholders so you fill specifics once, then rehearse out loud. You still own the truth of each story — the desk only stops you from improvising under stress.

Pair each story with one metric or outcome you can say in one breath. If you cannot name the result, revise the Situation and Task before you open the call.

Follow-ups that stay professional

After an interview, a short thank-you is expected. After radio silence, a calm nudge beats another “just checking in” essay. Hunt’s swipe file keeps tone steady: gratitude, one reminder of fit, and a clear ask for timeline — then stop. No fake urgency. No sales gimmicks.

Salary signals and status honesty

Log a salary number when the posting or recruiter shares one — even a range midpoint. Empty salary fields make offer comparisons emotional instead of numeric. Status should move when reality moves: Applied after you submit, Interview when a screen is booked, Rejected when you get the email — not when you feel discouraged. Hunt’s filters exist so you can focus on “Interview” rows this week without scrolling past old Saves.

FAQ

Is Hunt a job board?

No. It does not scrape listings or auto-apply. You paste roles you choose and track them offline.

Does my data leave my device?

No upload. Data stays in localStorage until you export. There is no account and no support channel.

Is this Notion?

The desk is offline HTML. The zip includes Notion-ready CSVs if you prefer a database later.
